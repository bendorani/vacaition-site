Delete your Vacaition account

App: Vacaition (developer: Vacaition)

Contact: hello@vacaition.app

In the app

Open Vacaition, tap your profile picture, then Settings, then Delete account. Confirm, and the deletion happens right away.

Without the app

Email hello@vacaition.app with the subject “Delete my account”. Send it from the email address you sign in with (Google or Apple). We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What we delete

Your account, profile, and all your trips, places, notes and reel links.

What we keep

Database backups are kept for up to 7 days, and then the deleted data is gone from them too. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and our payment provider (RevenueCat) as required by law. Active subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel them in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
