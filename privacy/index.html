1. Who we are

Vacaition (“we”, “us”) provides an app that helps you turn social travel videos and preferences into trip itineraries.

2. What we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

Data Why
Account info (e.g. name, email from Apple / Google Sign-In) Create and secure your account
Trip content you create (destinations, dates, places, notes, optional pins) Build, sync, and show your itineraries
Reel / social URLs you paste or share into the app Extract place suggestions for your trip
Device / app basics (app version, OS, approximate diagnostics) Keep the app reliable and fix bugs
Purchase / entitlement status (via Apple, Google, and/or RevenueCat, when enabled) Provide Vacaition Pro or a single-trip purchase if you buy one

We do not ask for your social network passwords. Sharing a public reel link is enough for extraction.

Location

We use place coordinates and maps to show and order stops on your trip. We do not continuously track your background location for advertising. If the OS asks for location permission, it is for map / trip features you use in the app.

Photos

If you upload trip photos (e.g. gallery), they are stored to show in your trip. We do not sell your photos.

3. How we use AI and third-party services

To build itineraries and understand reels we may send relevant content (e.g. place names, captions, trip context) to:

  • Our own API and workers
  • Map / places providers (e.g. Google Maps Platform)
  • AI language models (Anthropic Claude) to plan and edit trips — trip details you enter are sent to build your plan; they are not used to train the model

These providers process data under their terms to return results to us. We configure them for the trip-planning purpose above—not to sell your personal data.

4. Advertising and purchases

  • Free tier: we show ads (Google AdMob). Ad networks may collect device advertising identifiers and similar data under their policies. On iPhone we ask before using the advertising identifier; in the EU/UK we ask for consent first, and you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Vacaition Pro and single trips: if you buy, we use Apple / Google In-App Purchase and RevenueCat to check what you bought. Payment details stay with Apple/Google; we receive purchase status, not your card number.

5. Sharing

We share data only with:

  • Service providers that run the app (hosting, databases, maps, AI, analytics/ads/purchases as configured)
  • Authorities if required by law

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Retention

We keep account and trip data while your account is active. You can delete your account and associated trip data in the app (Profile → Delete account). You may also email hello@vacaition.app. Backups may persist for a limited period before deletion completes. Aggregated or anonymized data may be kept for improving the product.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures (e.g. HTTPS, access controls) to protect data in transit and at rest. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

8. Children

Vacaition is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Do not use the app if you are under that age.

9. International transfers

If you use the app from outside the region where our servers or providers operate, your data may be processed in other countries with different data-protection laws. We take steps appropriate to the services we use.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data, or object to certain processing. Contact hello@vacaition.app. You may also have the right to complain to a local data-protection authority.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the “Last updated” date and, when changes are material, provide a reasonable notice in the app or on the site.
